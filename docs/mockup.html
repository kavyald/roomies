Roomies

Five concepts: needs, chores, tasks, polls, and runs. Money is only recorded when something was spent. Names, numbers, and contacts are placeholders.

Things to try

  • 🛒Needs: add “tomatoes” twice, toggle Soon, or tap a circle for Got it.
  • 🧺Start a run from Needs with Select Soon, tick items, then Finish and log what you spent.
  • 🗳Polls: vote on “Which vacuum?” (Home → Open polls), then close it. The closed lamp poll shows a tie.
  • 📞Outside help: open “Bathroom 2 fan,” tap Needs outside help?, pick the super (or add someone new).
  • ➕Add a poll option to “Which vacuum?” after it started. Voters can switch.
  • 🔧Plan a visit (Tasks): the super, the radiator + fan, a time.
  • ⚖︎Feeling weights (House → Settings): set 😰 to +40 and watch the Home feed re-rank.
  • 💸Add cost on the vacuum once it’s bought. Tomatoes never ask.
